## Nomi 2.0.2

Nomi 2.0.2 combines the polished Material 3 refresh with a new personal estimate for calories burned through steps.

### What changed

- The complete visual rework is included: warmer colors, stronger typography, crisper surfaces and smoother transitions without moving familiar controls.
- Nomi now estimates active walking calories from today's steps and the latest saved weight, personalized with height when available.
- The estimate appears in the existing Today calorie pill, both goal-card styles and the Health Connect activity details.
- Values are visibly approximate and rounded to avoid false precision.
- Health Connect total active calories stay separate because they may also contain workouts, cycling or other activity.
- Step calories are not added to the food target or calories left for the day.
- Missing Health Connect calorie data is no longer presented as a measured zero.
- The calculation runs locally and the new labels are available in all ten supported interface languages.

### Accuracy note

The estimate models ordinary walking. Pace, incline, running, terrain, carrying weight, individual gait and step-count accuracy can change actual energy use.

### Compatibility

- Existing food logs, goals, profile data, provider settings and backups are unchanged.
- Android 8.0 and newer remain supported.
