# Nomi v2.10.0

### Setup and settings

**Your AI key**

- Setup now asks for the one thing Nomi cannot work without. The last step before your plan takes a Google Gemini key and an Exa key, checks them with a real request, and saves them. You can skip it; Today then says a key is missing and takes you to where it goes.
- Gemini + Exa is the recommended setup and the default on a new install: Exa finds the nutrition sources, and Gemini reads what you log, changes portions and looks at photos. One Gemini key covers all of it.
- Installs that were already set up keep their providers. Nothing is switched for you, and your stored keys keep working.
- An error that says "check it in Settings" now has a button that opens that page. Before, the first meal on a new install failed with a sentence pointing at a setting nobody had been shown.
- Error messages on Today appear in your language. They were shown in English whatever the app was set to.

**Settings**

- Everything about AI is behind one row, **AI provider**, which says whether it is ready. The page opens with the keys your setup needs: Gemini and Exa on the recommended setup, a single field when one provider runs everything.
- The provider and model of each task are still there, folded under "Provider and model per task". The calorie estimate bias, "Never time out" and the AI debug log moved to the same page.
- Theme, Dynamic colors and Goals view each have their own icon.

**Provider editor**

- It is a page instead of a dialog. Test connection and Save stay at the bottom while the form scrolls.
- The key field has a show/hide eye, and a link to the page where the provider issues keys.
- Known models are offered as one-tap suggestions, so an empty model field is no longer a dead end.
- Switching provider keeps a key you had already pasted, and shows whether the new provider already has one stored.
- The address of a built-in provider is no longer shown as a field you cannot type in.

**Onboarding**

- Continue is pinned to the bottom of every step. It used to be the last row of the list, so it scrolled away on long questions and went under the keyboard on typed ones. Start tracking is pinned the same way.
- A refused answer is explained directly above the button you tapped.

**History**

- History matches the rest of the app. Each day is a card with its calories against the target, a progress bar and its carbs, protein and fat, on the same tinted page as Progress and Settings.
- The foods of a day are written the way Today writes them, and a picked food is highlighted as a whole row.
- "Add items to today" and "Save meal" confirm in a floating bar at the bottom instead of a strip across the screen.
- An empty history and a search without results each say so, with the fox.

**Health Connect and AI debug**

- Both pages use the same rows and cards as the rest of Settings.
- Recording debug events is a switch. It was a button whose label flipped between Enable and Disable.

Screens that take a key stay out of screenshots and the recent-apps preview.

Install the attached APK over your existing Nomi installation to keep your data. Your stored keys, providers, food log, settings, and calorie targets are unchanged.
