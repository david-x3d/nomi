## Nomi 2.0

Nomi 2.0 makes everyday logging feel more responsive, polished and natural while keeping the familiar notes-first experience.

### What changed

- Today has a softer dynamic-colour header, a subtle fox halo and an elevated floating action dock.
- Holding a food opens its rounded quick actions beside that entry instead of against the screen edge.
- Meaningful haptic feedback and press animations now accompany the app's most important interactions.
- Multi-item inputs name the researched foods instead of becoming a generic menu label.
- For example, `250g tenderloin 120g pommes und ein red bull` appears as `Tenderloin mit Pommes und Red Bull`.
- Corrected item names are joined with language-aware words and punctuation, while each item's amount, nutrition and sources remain available in the meal details.
