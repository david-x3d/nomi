## Nomi 1.8

Nomi now feels as responsive as it looks.

### What changed

- Meaningful haptic feedback now accompanies navigation, date changes, settings, capture actions, submitting, saving, errors, swipe-to-delete, Undo and quick actions.
- Frequently used circular actions press inward and spring back, and the selected destination icon receives a small expressive lift.
- Barcode recognition confirms itself with a tactile response and a green scan frame before the amount sheet opens.
- Hold any food on Today for quick access to Duplicate, Change amount, Favorite and Delete.
- Food analysis clearly moves through four stages: understanding the meal, finding nutrition, checking portions and putting the result together.
- Research-source icons remain visible while Nomi checks nutrition.
- Compact Today actions include tooltips, and an empty day offers a friendly example to help you begin.

Haptics follow the device's system feedback settings.
