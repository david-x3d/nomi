## Nomi 1.9 Beta 1

This beta refreshes how Nomi feels without turning it into a different app.

### What changed

- The Today header has a soft dynamic-colour wash that follows Nomi's current state without judging your day.
- The Nomi fox sits in a subtle matching halo, with a clearer wordmark and the same compact footprint.
- Calories, voice, camera and library actions now live in one elevated floating dock above the notes page.
- Holding a food opens its rounded quick-action menu beside that entry instead of against the left edge of the screen.
- Existing notes-first behaviour, gestures, dynamic colour, dark theme and pitch-black support remain unchanged.

### Beta note

This is a visual and interaction preview. It does not migrate or change saved nutrition data, AI providers or research behaviour.
