# Nomi v2.10.5

### Preferred OpenRouter provider for every task

- The preferred OpenRouter provider now applies to every request Nomi sends through OpenRouter, not only Exa + OpenRouter. That covers food research, reading what you log, portion changes, photos and the fallback.
- The field is easier to find. It has its own OpenRouter section under Settings → AI provider whenever any task uses OpenRouter or Exa + OpenRouter. It also appears in the provider editor of every OpenRouter task.
- Enter a provider such as "baseten", and OpenRouter asks it first. If that provider is unavailable, OpenRouter uses the next one. Leave the field blank to let OpenRouter choose. The price ceiling for Exa + OpenRouter stays as before.

Install the attached APK over your existing Nomi installation to keep your data.
