# Nomi v2.8.0

### Where do the calories come from?

- The nutrition detail page has a new section that explains every entry in the language the app is set to, not only in English.
- **Data origin**: whether the values came from a published nutrition table, Open Food Facts, your photo of a label, a Nomi estimate, your food library, your own input or another Nomi, with the website, the product on the page and the serving the values describe. A combined meal shows the origin of each food.
- **Calorie composition**: a bar and one row per macronutrient (grams × kcal per gram, kcal and share), the macro sum next to the logged total, and the calculation from the source's serving to your portion.
- **Good to know**: the main calorie driver, energy density, protein share, why a total can differ from its macros, the largest part of a meal, and a note on estimates.
- Everything is worked out on the phone from what was saved with the entry, so it works for entries logged before this version too.

### Translations

- Error and status messages from AI providers, AI answer checks, speech recognition, photos, Health Connect, onboarding and backups now appear in all ten languages, including messages that carry a value such as an HTTP status or a provider name.
- The Health Connect privacy page and the package-size note are no longer limited to German and English.
- Source names such as "Manual entry" and "Nomi favorite" are shown translated.

Install the attached APK over your existing Nomi installation to keep your data.
