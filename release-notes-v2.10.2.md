# Nomi v2.10.2

### Activity target

- The "Adjust target from activity" switch now adds Nomi's estimated step calories to today's food calorie target. The target, remaining calories, and progress update as steps change; the goal card shows the base target plus the step estimate.
- Health Connect active calories are not added on top of the step estimate. Days without a step estimate and past days keep their planned target. The saved nutrition plan and macro goals stay unchanged.
- This replacement APK has a higher Android version code and installs over the first v2.10.2 APK.

### History

- Fix the empty search message using dark text on a gray background with some Material color schemes. The History page and its "Nothing found" heading now use the active theme's readable foreground color.

Install the attached APK over your existing Nomi installation to keep your data.
