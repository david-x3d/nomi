# Nomi v2.0.6

This release adds Nomi to the home screen.

- Two widgets: a 2x2 showing today's calories against your target, and a 4x2 that adds protein, carbohydrate and fat progress.
- Both read today's totals and your active nutrition plan, open Nomi when tapped, and show a setup hint until onboarding is finished.
- They refresh when your data changes, at midnight, and after a reboot or time-zone change, without waking the app to redraw numbers that have not moved.
- Every widget string is translated across all nine supported languages, and the two widgets are now named separately in the widget picker.
- The calorie figure no longer loses its lower edge, and the fat bar is no longer cut off the 4x2 widget: the macros sit in three side-by-side columns that fit a 4x2 cell.
- Visual pass in the Material 3 Expressive direction: a heavier hero numeral, a larger surface radius, and thicker fully rounded progress bars.

The APK is signed with the same certificate as prior stable releases.
